Privacy policy

Last updated: November 8, 2026.

This OAuth client is intended for personal access to the owner's Google Drive accounts using rclone on the owner's computer. Google Drive access is requested when the account holder grants permission through Google.

Rclone handles data locally and communicates with Google Drive to perform operations initiated by the owner. This setup does not operate a separate application server. Google handles account authorization and Drive storage under its own terms and policies.

The account holder can revoke the client's access in their Google Account's third-party connections. Revocation stops further access through that authorization; it does not automatically delete previously copied files.
